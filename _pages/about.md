I am **Ziyu Huang (黄子昱)**, a Ph.D. student in Computer Science and Technology at Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU), starting from 2023.

My academic journey has spanned several disciplines and institutions. I completed a Master of Science in Computer Science and Technology at the National University of Singapore (NUS) from 2021 to 2023. Prior to that, I earned a Master of Science in Mechanical Engineering from UCLA (2020-2021).

I received my Bachelor's degree from the Harbin Institute of Technology (HIT) in 2020, where I was part of the School of Astronautics' Experimental Class, focusing on Engineering Mechanics and Composite Materials.
